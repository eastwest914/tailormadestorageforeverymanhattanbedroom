Our Story

Craftsmanship That Fits Your Home, Not the Other Way Around

Custom Wardrobes Midtown NYC is a local cabinet maker focused on bedroom storage. We measure your room, listen to how you live, and build wardrobes that use every inch of floor, wall and ceiling height.

Whether you want a sleek modern finish, an Italian-inspired look or a fully fitted walk-in with built-in organizers, we guide you from first sketch to final installation across Manhattan.
